Zen GolfPrivacy Policy

What happens on your phone stays on your phone.

Zen Golf is a pre-shot meditation app for golfers. This page explains, plainly, what that means for your data.

Last updated September 27, 2026

The short version: Zen Golf collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ad networks, and no servers — the app never sends anything to us. The only thing that ever goes online is optional iCloud sync, and only if you turn it on.

Your swing notes and settings live in your device's local storage, the same way a note-taking app might keep a draft before you save it. If you turn on iCloud sync, your notes and settings are stored in your own iCloud account through Apple; we never receive them. Either way, we never see any of it.

01 What we collect

Nothing. Zen Golf does not ask for your name, email, location, or any account credentials, because it has no sign-up and no servers to send them to.

We don't use analytics SDKs, crash reporters, advertising identifiers, or any third-party tracking libraries. There is no code in this app whose job is to observe you.

02 What's stored on your device

The app keeps a few things locally, using standard on-device storage (iOS's AsyncStorage), purely so the app remembers your preferences between sessions:

SWING NOTES
The personal cues you write to remember before each round.
ACTIVITY DATES
The days you completed a pre-shot session. Kept only on your device.
PREFERENCES
Session length, accent color, appearance, note text size, and reveal style (funny or inspirational).

None of this is bundled or uploaded to us. It's stored the same way as any other app's local settings — private to your device and inaccessible to us.

Optional iCloud sync. If you turn on iCloud sync, your notes and settings are stored in your own iCloud account through Apple; we never receive them. Sync is off by default and uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage, which Apple's own privacy policy covers. Turning it off in the app's Settings stops syncing.

03 Deleting your data

Since everything lives on your device, you're always in full control. Deleting a swing note in the app removes it immediately. Uninstalling Zen Golf deletes all of it at once — there's no copy sitting with us to ask us to erase. If you turned on iCloud sync, a copy of your notes and settings also stays in your own iCloud account, which Apple manages for you.

04 Children's privacy

Zen Golf does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, for the simple reason that it doesn't collect information from anyone. The app is suitable for golfers of all ages who want a calmer pre-shot routine.

05 Changes to this policy

If Zen Golf ever changes in a way that affects this policy — for instance, if a future version adds an optional cloud backup — this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top, before that change ships.
